A Lunar Week

The Tranquility itinerary

Our most popular voyage. Nine days door to door, five of them in the Sea of Tranquility.

01

Lift-off

Brunch on the gantry, then eight and a half minutes that will rearrange your understanding of the word "fast".

02

Coast

Three days in the Clipper lounge. Cocktails with spheres. Earth shrinking to the size of a dinner plate, then a dime.

04

Touchdown

Descent to Hotel Tranquility, 1.6 km from the original landing site (viewing gallery only — no souvenirs).

06

Rover day

A pressurised rover picnic to the rim of crater Moltke. Lunch served in one-sixth gravity; soup is not advised.

09

Splashdown

Re-entry at 11 km/s and a warm welcome on the recovery ship. Certificate, medal, and a very long nap.
