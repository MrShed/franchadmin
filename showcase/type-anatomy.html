Fraunces is by Phaedra Charles and Flavia Zimbardi (Undercase Type), a "Wonky" Old Style inspired by early-20th-century faces such as Windsor, Souvenir and Cooper. Recursive is by Stephen Nixon (Arrow Type), a mono-to-sans family drawn from single-stroke casual brush lettering. Both are open source and served by Google Fonts. Anatomy positions are measured from the rendered glyphs at run time.
