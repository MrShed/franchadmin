  1. No cladding.If a wall is concrete, it looks like concrete — inside and out. Every surface you see is structural or it is not there.
  2. Formwork is drawing.We specify every board of rough-sawn Douglas fir: its width, direction, and joint. The texture is designed, not an accident.
  3. Light is poured too.We cast openings deep: 600 mm reveals that turn daylight into a material with an edge.
  4. Build for 300 years.The greenest building is the one that never needs replacing. We use low-clinker mixes and design for the second, third and fourth use.

Method

01

Ground

Six months on site before a line is drawn. We log sun, wind, water and the paths people already walk.

02

Mass

Clay models at 1:200, carved not assembled. We look for the one block the whole building wants to be.

03

Pour

Full-size test panels on site. The client chooses the board, the aggregate and the day's weather they prefer.

04

Weather

We return every five years to see how the rain has drawn on the walls — and learn from it.
