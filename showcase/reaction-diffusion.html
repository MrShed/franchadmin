Morphologies of the Gray–Scott System, grown in vitro upon a glass plate

Two chemicals, U and V, diffuse and react: U + 2V → 3V. Feed and kill rates alone decide whether the dish grows coral, divides like cells, or whorls into fingerprints. Draw on the glass to inoculate.
