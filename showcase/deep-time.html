The Big Five

Five times in the last half-billion years, most species on Earth vanished in a geological instant. Bars show the share of species lost, as usually estimated from the marine fossil record; the pale extension marks the range where estimates disagree.

One day on Earth

Compress 4.54 billion years into 24 hours and each second holds 52,546 years. Animals turn up in the evening; the whole of written history fits in the last tenth of a second.
