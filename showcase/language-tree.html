The Codex

Every leaf of the tree, set out plainly. Cognates of the Indo-European roots in gold.

Forms are given in native script where one is standard, with a romanisation. Some languages have several everyday words (Hindi mā̃ and mātā; Japanese haha and okāsan); the entry shows a common neutral form. Cognacy follows standard etymological dictionaries; Romanian mamă, Welsh mam and many "mama"-type words across unrelated families are nursery words, not inherited from *méh₂tēr. Language counts per family are rounded Ethnologue/Glottolog figures.
