How the ice works

  • Walk with the arrow keys, WASD, the pad or a swipe.
  • Walk into a block to kick it. It slides until something stops it. Pip stays put.
  • Snowdrifts stop a sliding block dead.
  • Undo with Z or U, restart with R.
