The House

Grown, then worn

We are gardeners before we are perfumers. Every fragrance starts as a bed we plant, and ends in a bottle eighteen months later.

Isobel Marr and Théo Castagne founded Salvia & Lilas in 2014 in a Victorian kitchen garden outside Edinburgh, with a copper alembic bought from a retiring lavender farmer in Valensole. Today half of our materials come from our own beds; the rest from four growers we visit every harvest.

Our fragrances are composed in a single sitting and then left alone for six months to settle. Nothing synthetic is used to imitate what the garden already makes.
