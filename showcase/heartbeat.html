Atrial systole. The P wave spreads across the atria; they squeeze the last fifth or so of the blood down through the open tricuspid and mitral valves.
Ventricular systole. The QRS fires the ventricles. Rising pressure slams the AV valves shut — the first sound, lub — then forces open the pulmonary and aortic valves.
Diastole. After the T wave the ventricles relax; the semilunar valves snap closed — dub — and the AV valves fall open to refill.
