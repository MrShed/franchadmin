The Label

Two inks. One take.

We record live to two-track in a single room with no headphones. What the band played is what you get.

Low Tide started in 2011 when bassist Colm Reddy bought a 1962 lathe from a closing BBC studio and set it up in a boathouse on the Shannon estuary. The first release, pressed in an edition of 300, sold out at a single gig.

We still work the same way. Sessions happen on Sunday afternoons when the tide is out and the boathouse is quiet. Sleeves are screen-printed by Aoife Brennan in two inks — always two — on uncoated board, and every record is inspected by ear before it's posted.
