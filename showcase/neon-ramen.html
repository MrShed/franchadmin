03 / 由来 Story

Kazuhiro Tanabe's grandmother opened the stall in 2061, three years before the Namba arcades were sunk underground to escape the summer heat. She named it Yūrei — ghost — because the steam off her pot drifted down the corridor and pulled strangers in like a haunting.

Everything upstairs changed. Delivery drones replaced delivery bikes; the canal lights went holographic. Kaz still skims his pot by hand at 3 a.m., and still refuses to let the ordering machine speak. You press a button. It prints a ticket. That's it.
