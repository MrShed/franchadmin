01 — fragebogen

kandinsky's questionnaire, 1923

in the wall-painting workshop at weimar, wassily kandinsky handed out a single sheet of paper. it showed a triangle, a square and a circle, all empty, and asked the reader to fill each one with the colour it most naturally called for — and, if possible, to say why.

he believed the answer was universal: the sharp, active triangle is yellow; the calm, earthbound square is red; the deep, inward circle is blue. most of the students agreed. many historians since have noted that students might have known what the master wanted to hear.

03 — geschichte

fourteen years, three cities

the bauhaus lasted only from 1919 to 1933, never had more than about two hundred students at a time, and was pushed out of two cities before the nazis forced it shut. it still shaped almost every chair, typeface and apartment block of the century that followed.

1919

weimar

walter gropius merges the grand-ducal academy of art with the school of arts and crafts. the founding manifesto, with lyonel feininger's woodcut of a crystalline cathedral, calls for artists and craftsmen to build "the building of the future" together.

johannes itten's mystical vorkurs teaches materials, contrast and breathing exercises. the 1923 exhibition — "art and technology: a new unity" — marks the turn toward industry.

1925

dessau

squeezed out by weimar's conservative government, the school moves to industrial dessau. gropius designs the glass-curtained school building and the masters' houses.

workshops now license products: marianne brandt's lamps, marcel breuer's tubular-steel chairs, gunta stölzl's weaving. herbert bayer sets everything in lowercase. hannes meyer, director from 1928, pushes social housing.

1932

berlin

after the nazi-dominated city council closes the dessau school, ludwig mies van der rohe reopens it privately in an empty telephone factory in berlin-steglitz.

the gestapo seals the building in april 1933; the masters vote to dissolve in july. the diaspora — to chicago, black mountain college, harvard, tel aviv — carries its ideas around the world.

05 — grundsätze

what they believed

the workshop, not the academy

every student learned a craft under two masters — one of form, one of craft. making came before theorising.

gesamtkunstwerk

architecture as the total work of art: furniture, textiles, lamps, typography and murals designed as one environment.

truth to materials

steel should look like steel and wood like wood. ornament hides a material; construction shows it off.

design for industry

prototypes for mass production, so that good design would be cheap enough for the worker's flat.

elementary form

circle, square, triangle; red, blue, yellow; black and white. reduce the vocabulary until everything left is essential.

one alphabet

bayer's universal typeface abolished capitals: "we don't speak a capital a and a small a — why write two alphabets?" this page, in tribute, does the same.
