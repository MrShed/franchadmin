01

From easel to factory

история · history
Down with art as a sacred object. Long live art as work, as construction, as everyday life.The spirit of the First Working Group of Constructivists, Moscow, 1921 (paraphrased)

Constructivism began with Vladimir Tatlin's counter-reliefs of 1914 — assemblages of iron, glass and wood hung in the corners of rooms — and exploded after the 1917 Revolution. A new state wanted a new visual language, and a generation of artists believed that painting pictures for the bourgeoisie was over.

In 1921 the First Working Group of Constructivists, led by Aleksei Gan, Aleksandr Rodchenko and Varvara Stepanova, declared the end of "pure" art. The artist would design furniture, textiles, workers' clothing, books, advertisements, stage sets and architecture — anything useful. The poster became the frontline.

They built a graphic grammar still used everywhere: heavy sans-serif type set on diagonals, photographs cut and collaged into new meanings, a palette of red, black and paper, and compositions that feel like they are moving even on a wall. By 1932 Stalin's decree on literary and artistic organisations had pushed it all aside for Socialist Realism, but the language had already escaped to Berlin, Dessau, Paris and the rest of the century.

1914

Tatlin shows his first counter-reliefs after visiting Picasso's studio in Paris.

1919

Lissitzky's Beat the Whites with the Red Wedge; Tatlin designs his Monument to the Third International.

1920

VKhUTEMAS, the Higher Art and Technical Studios, open in Moscow — the Soviet counterpart to the Bauhaus.

1922

Aleksei Gan publishes Konstruktivizm, the movement's manifesto.

1924

Rodchenko's Books poster: Lilya Brik shouting a cone of letters: books in all branches of knowledge.

1932

Independent artistic groups are dissolved by decree. Socialist Realism becomes the only official style.

03

Principles of construction

принципы · principles
↗

The diagonal

Horizontal is rest; vertical is monument; the diagonal is motion. Everything leans into the future.

■

Faktura

The honest texture of a material — steel, wood, paper, ink — shown rather than hidden.

◐

Photomontage

The camera replaces the brush. Cut, paste and combine photographs to build new realities.

A

Type as image

Letters are heavy, bold and structural — they are part of the composition, not captions to it.

3

Three colours

Red, black, and the paper itself. Maximum contrast from the cheapest printing.

⚙

Tectonics

Form follows the logic of construction and the needs of its use, like a machine.

!

Agitation

Art is a tool that speaks to the masses: in kiosks, on trains, on biscuit wrappers.

∞

Art into life

No separation between fine and applied art. The artist is an engineer of daily life.
