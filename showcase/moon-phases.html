The twelve months

Each small moon shows the phase at about 9 pm UK time that evening. Shaded squares mark a principal phase; hover or focus for the exact time.

Thirteen full moons

2026 fits thirteen full moons into twelve months, so May has two — the second is a “Blue Moon”. Names follow the traditional North American and English folk calendar popularised by almanacs; the Harvest Moon is the full moon nearest the September equinox.

Method: principal phases from Meeus, Astronomical Algorithms (2nd ed.), ch. 49, including the fourteen planetary correction terms, typically accurate to under a minute; ΔT taken as 69 s. Nightly illumination from the ch. 48 phase-angle formula; distance from the leading terms of the ch. 47 lunar series (accurate to a few dozen km). Eclipse dates are from published NASA predictions. Times are converted to Europe/London by your browser.
