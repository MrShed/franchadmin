Every sound is synthesized on the fly: sine-sweep kick, noise-and-tone snare, six detuned square waves for the hats and cowbell, and a triple-burst clap. There are no samples. Keys: Space start or stop, ↑ ↓ choose an instrument, 1–8 and Q–I toggle steps, M mute. To keep a pattern, press Store and then a letter.
