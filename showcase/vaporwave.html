Welcome, Shopperようこそ · a short history of vaporwave

Vaporwave is a music genre, a visual style and an in-joke that grew up on the early-2010s internet. Producers took the background sound of late capitalism — smooth jazz, hold music, 80s commercials, department-store muzak — and slowed it, looped it and drenched it in reverb until it sounded like a memory of a shopping trip you never took.

The visuals followed the sound: Roman busts beside early 3D renders, Japanese text nobody on the album could read, dolphins, palm trees, grid horizons, and the grey windows of mid-90s operating systems. Glossy corporate optimism, seen from the other side of its collapse.

Its favourite setting is the dead mall — the half-empty American shopping centre, fountain still running, escalators still moving, stores shuttered one by one. Mallsoft, a subgenre, set out to recreate that sound exactly: echoes, footsteps, a PA chime, a saxophone from three floors away.

  1. 2010 — Daniel Lopatin, as Chuck Person, releases Chuck Person's Eccojams Vol. 1: chopped, slowed pop loops. The blueprint.
  2. 2011 — James Ferraro's Far Side Virtual turns ringtones and stock-software sounds into a satire of consumer tech.
  3. 2011 — Vektroid, as Macintosh Plus, releases Floral Shoppe. A marble bust of Helios on a pink grid becomes the genre's emblem; "リサフランク420 / 現代のコンピュー" its anthem.
  4. 2013 — Blank Banshee, Saint Pepsi and the rise of future funk.
  5. 2015 — 猫 シ Corp.'s Palm Mall defines mallsoft: an entire album set inside an imaginary shopping centre.
  6. 2016– — The aesthetic escapes: fashion, memes, album art, and eventually brand campaigns — the very thing it parodied.

Daniel Lopatin

チャック・パーソン

As Chuck Person and Oneohtrix Point Never, he found the eerie beauty in a slowed-down 80s pop hook.

Vektroid

マッキントッシュ・プラス

Ramona Andra Xavier. Dozens of aliases; Floral Shoppe is the most famous vaporwave album ever made.

James Ferraro

ファー・サイド

Hypnagogic pop pioneer; his hyper-clean digital satires shaped the genre's look and irony.

猫 シ Corp.

ネコ・シ・コープ

The mallsoft architect — field recordings of fountains and PA systems, looped into dreams.

Blank Banshee

ブランク・バンシー

Brought trap drums and crisp production; his album art pushed the 3D-glitch visual style.

Helios

ヘーリオス

A plaster bust of the sun god, photographed off a museum website, now the unofficial face of an entire internet movement.
