12.2 Scoring

Each bite scores the current level number, so level 9 pays nine times more than level 1. After every fifth bite a critter appears in the field. Catch it before the counter in the top-right corner reaches zero and you keep whatever is left on the counter.

Your best result is kept in the phone's memory as Top score, even after you switch the phone off.

12.3 Tip

Choose level 5 or higher for serious scores. The display has a slow response, so fast snakes leave a faint trail. That is normal and does not mean the phone is faulty.

Koivu is a fictional handset. The display is emulated at its native 84 × 48 pixels and scaled up. Nothing is stored except your top score.
