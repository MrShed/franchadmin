    9,192,631,770

    Since 1967 the second has been defined as exactly this many cycles of the radiation from the hyperfine transition of the caesium-133 atom. Optical clocks now tick about a hundred thousand times faster and beat caesium by more than a hundredfold in stability — so metrologists are preparing to redefine the second again, with the 2030 General Conference on Weights and Measures the target date.

    Sources: NPL (Essen & Parry, 1955); NIST (F1, F2 fountains); JILA (Sr lattice clock, 8.1 × 10⁻¹⁹ systematic uncertainty, 2024); Royal Museums Greenwich (H4 trial 1761–62); Seiko (Astron 35SQ, ±5 s/month). Ancient and medieval values are order-of-magnitude estimates.
